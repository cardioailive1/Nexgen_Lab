FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between plan tokens and credits?
Your subscription plan includes a monthly token allowance (e.g. Pro includes 20 million tokens). These reset at the start of each billing cycle and cannot roll over. Credits are a separate, prepaid balance that never expires — they only get drawn down once your plan's monthly tokens are exhausted. This means your subscription handles predictable baseline usage while credits handle overflow or variable workloads.
How does auto-reload work?
When auto-reload is enabled, we monitor your credit balance and automatically charge your card on file to top up to the configured amount whenever it drops below the threshold you set. For example: top up with $50 when balance drops below $25. You can disable auto-reload at any time and the next top-up will not be processed. Recurring charges appear as "Corverxis Technologies — NexGen Credits" on your statement.
Which model should I use?
NexGen Flash is best for high-volume applications where speed and cost matter — chatbots, search, classification. NexGen Pro is best for code generation, research, and tasks requiring careful reasoning. NexGen Ultra is for the most demanding tasks: deep domain analysis, complex multi-step reasoning, and enterprise applications where accuracy is critical. If you're unsure, start with Flash and upgrade as needed.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel at any time from your account billing settings. On monthly plans, you retain access until the end of the billing period. On annual plans, remaining months are not refunded but you keep access for the full year. Any unused credit balance remains available after cancellation and never expires.
How do I get an API key?
Once you have a paid plan, log into the NexGen Frontier Lab and navigate to API Dev → Keys. Click "Generate API key", give it a name, and copy the nxg-... key shown. This is the only time the full key is displayed — store it securely. Use it in any API call as the Authorization header: Authorization: Bearer nxg-...
Is there a free trial?
Yes — the Free plan gives you 100,000 tokens per month on NexGen Flash with no credit card required. This is enough to build and test most integrations. Upgrade to a paid plan when you're ready to go to production or need access to Pro/Ultra models.
